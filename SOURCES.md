# Sources

Every number in Hold'em Trainer is either **computed by the app's own engine** (exact enumeration or combinatorics, unit-tested) or **traced to a source below**. Sources were first checked on **2026-11-19**; every chart source was re-fetched and re-compared on **2026-11-23** (see AUDIT.md).

Information is labelled in the UI as one of three kinds:

| Label | Meaning | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Math fact** | Exact, computed by code | `src/engine/*`, cross-checked against the references in §1 |
| **Theory baseline** | Published charts / theory, a model with assumptions | §2 (charts) and §3 (concepts) |
| **Practical advice** | Exploitative, home-game advice; not math | Written for this app, always labelled and hideable (Settings → Home-game exploit notes) |

## 4. Scenario grading

- **Chart spots** (procedural preflop, and library spots marked `"basis": "chart"`): graded against the charts in §2. Mixed actions → "Acceptable".
- **Math spots** (procedural postflop, and library spots marked `"basis": "equity"`): equity vs a **stated** villain range compared with required equity B/(P+2B); ±3 points counts as close (both call and fold acceptable). The assumed range is shown with every spot.
- **Checked-to spots** (procedural c-bet / barrel / river, and Full hand when the villain checks): the **value rule** — bet when your equity vs the hands that would call is above 50%; otherwise check. Bluffs are acceptable with little showdown value or a strong draw; small protection bets with medium strength are acceptable on the flop (and turn while ahead). A spot can be flagged `noValueRaise` when the villain's range is polarized (raising only folds his bluffs).
- **Full hand mode**: the villain's hand is dealt at random from his opening range and he acts with fixed probabilities by hand-strength class (table shown in the app, `src/scenarios/fullhand.ts`). After each action his range is re-weighted by the probability of that action, and decisions are graded as above. **This strategy is a modelling assumption written for this app, not solver output or a published source.**
- **Authored spots** (`"basis": "authored"`): written rationale based on the theory in §3, with engine numbers (equity vs the stated range) filled into the text. `tests/library.test.ts` recomputes every stored number and checks that "call"/"fold" recommendations are consistent with the price unless the spot is explicitly an implied-odds call.

## 6. Exact preflop equity table (Range Explorer, bot review, 169-hand ranking)

`src/data/preflopTable.json` stores, for each of the **47,008** heads-up preflop matchups that are distinct up to suit relabeling and seat swap, the exact count `2·wins + ties` over all **1,712,304** boards (C(48,5)). It was generated by `scripts/gen-preflop-table.ts` (8 parallel shards, the app's own evaluator) and is verified by `tests/preflopTable.test.ts`:
- all 47,008 entries present; seat swap gives the complement;
- 40 random matchups equal a fresh exhaustive enumeration (to 1e-9);
- the DeucesCracked published exact matchups (§1) agree to 0.05 points.
Equity of any two specific hands is exact; a range's equity is the weighted average over the opponent combos that don't conflict with your cards (card removal).
`src/data/preflopVsRandom.json` (equity of each of the 169 hands vs a random hand, and the strength order used by the bot and the Explorer) is now derived exactly from this table (`scripts/gen-vs-random-exact.ts`; AA = 85.20%, matching the independent full enumeration in §1).

## 8. Play a Hand (bot) and Range Explorer — models and defaults

These are **modelling assumptions written for this app**, documented in code and in the UI; none is solver output or a published source:
- **Bot preflop:** the cited RFI and vs-open charts (§2). Where no verified chart exists (vs-open spots other than BB vs BTN / BTN vs CO, every vs-3-bet spot, the BB facing a limp, the heads-up game) it uses documented heuristics based on the 169-hand strength order (§6): e.g. 3-bet the top 4% (solid style), call inside a band sized from the opener's chart (×2.0 from the BB, ×0.7 in position, ×0.5 out of position), 4-bet the top 2.5%, call a 3-bet with the top ~12%. The SB "raise or call" chart has no published split → 50/50.
- **Bot postflop:** Monte Carlo equity (6,000 trials, seeded) vs its estimate of your range; buckets (strong / medium / draw / air); calls when equity ≥ B/(P+2B) with +4 points for strong draws on flop/turn (calling station +8); MDF guard vs bets ≤ 40% pot; river bluff frequency set so bluffs make up B/(P+2B) of its betting range assuming a 1 : 1.5 value-to-air mix; sizes 33/50/75/100%/all-in by board texture and SPR.
- **Range estimates (both directions):** `src/bot/ranges.ts` (RANGE_RULES, POSTFLOP_MULT) — preflop ranges from the charts or the strength order, postflop multipliers by hand-strength class per action.
- **Styles:** solid / calling station / maniac are parameter presets — approximations of player types, not data-derived models.
- **Range Explorer model inputs:** villain response to a raise (fold % or "continues with his top N%" ranked by the preflop strength order or made-hand strength on the board, plus a re-raise share after which you fold); defaults: preflop the opener continues vs a 3-bet with his top 55% (20% of that 4-bets), postflop a betting range continues vs a raise 65% (20% re-raises); equity realization 0.8 for preflop calls, 0.9 for postflop calls, 1.0 after your raise is called — rough assumptions, adjustable, not sourced numbers.

**Multiway bot heuristics (written for this app — labelled "heuristic" in the bot log):**
- Equity vs all remaining opponents' estimated ranges together: Monte Carlo, 2,500 trials, 250 ms time budget per decision (shown with ≈).
- Fair share = 1 / players in the pot. Value threshold = fair share × (1.5 + 0.15 per extra opponent): heads-up 75%, 3-way 55%, 4-way 45%, 5-way 39%, 6-way 35%.
- Bluff, semi-bluff, stab and thin-value frequencies × 0.5 per extra opponent.
- Facing a bet: required equity = amount to call ÷ pot after calling, plus 3 equity points per player still to act behind. The MDF guard (keep pairs/draws vs bets ≤ 40% pot) applies heads-up only.
- Preflop where charts don't apply: iso-raise over limpers with the top 12% (to 3bb + 1 per limper); over-limp pairs and suited hands from the opening range; overcall an open after callers with the heads-up calling band ×0.7 per caller, plus pairs and suited connectors when ≥ 40bb deep; squeeze with the top max(2%, 3-bet% × (1 − 0.25 per caller)) to 3× the open + 1 open per caller (+1 out of position); cold-call a 3-bet with the top 5% (× 0.8 per caller), 4-bet the top 2%.
- Range tracking per opponent uses `src/bot/ranges.ts` (adds "iso", "overcall", "squeeze" preflop rules).
- **Home-game mix:** opponents take styles in the order calling station, solid, maniac, solid, solid — a guess at a typical home game, not data.
- **Action table:** opening and calling ranges ×1.35 by combos — a training convenience, not realistic play.
- **Review grading multiway:** facing a bet → equity vs combined ranges vs price, close within 3 points or within 6 points when players are still to act behind; no bet → bet is "best" only above the multiway value threshold, check is "best" below 0.7 × fair share (bluffing into 3+ players = mistake, 2 players = acceptable), everything else "close / depends on reads".
